TapCard Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 25, 2026

Overview

TapCard ("the App", "we") shows a translation card for a word you tap inside another app on your Android device. To read that word, the App captures the screen on your device and recognises text locally. The screen image never leaves your device. To fill the card in, the App sends only the word you tapped and the sentence around it to dictionary, translation and picture services.

There are no accounts, no ads, no advertising identifiers and no analytics in the App.

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location or advertising identifiers. The App contains no analytics SDK and no crash reporting.

Screen Capture

Android shows a system consent dialog before any capture begins — every time you turn the mode on. That consent cannot be cached. While the mode is running, Android shows a persistent notification and a recording indicator.

The App grabs a single frame at the moment you tap the floating bubble. That frame is handed to on-device text recognition, used to place the highlight boxes over the words, and then discarded. It is never uploaded, never written to storage and never shown to anyone.

Capture stops when you turn the mode off, when the screen locks, or when you revoke the permission.

Display Over Other Apps

The App draws a floating bubble and the word card above whatever you are reading. The overlay only draws — by itself it reads nothing from other apps.

Diagnostics Log

The App keeps a short in-memory log of the last few hundred events — scan timings, dictionary lookups, network failures — so that problems can be diagnosed. It contains the words you looked up. It is not written to storage and is never sent anywhere automatically; it is cleared when the App stops. You can view it inside the App and choose to share it yourself, in which case it goes only where you send it.

Children

TapCard is not directed to children. Dictionary entries come from Wiktionary and illustrations come from Pexels; neither is filtered by us, so definitions and images may be unsuitable for minors.

Data Deletion

We hold no account and no profile for you. The word and its sentence are processed in transit and are not retained. To have the device fingerprint removed from our backend, write to the address below and include the fingerprint shown in the App's diagnostics log.

Changes
