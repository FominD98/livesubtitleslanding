Telegram Live Translation & AI Voice Chat Captions

Telegram is no longer just a messenger — it is a broadcast platform with public channels reaching millions, voice chats running for hours, and supergroups where thousands of members chat across two dozen languages at once. The conversation has shifted heavily toward audio: voice messages, voice chats inside groups and channels, video calls, and audio-only town halls. Telegram's own translation features cover text reasonably well but stop at audio — there is no built-in real-time translation for voice chats or audio messages. Telegram live translation via Live Subtitles fills that gap. The app captions and translates any Telegram audio playing on your Windows PC in real time, in 50+ languages, with no bot to invite and no channel admin permission to obtain.

How to add live translation to Telegram in 3 steps

  1. Download Live Subtitles from the Microsoft Store and launch it alongside Telegram Desktop.
  2. Select "System Audio" — captures Telegram call audio and voice messages automatically.
  3. Join a voice chat or play an audio message — captions appear in a floating overlay on top of Telegram.

Voice chats in groups and channels

Voice chats turned Telegram into something like a cross between a podcast platform and a chat room — group voice chats running 24/7, channel-attached voice chats with hosts and listeners, and ad-hoc audio town halls in supergroups. They are often multilingual: a Russian crypto channel where speakers switch between Russian and English; a Ukrainian news supergroup where residents share updates in Ukrainian, Russian, and Polish; an Arabic political channel where guests dial in from across the region speaking different dialects.

Live Subtitles handles all of these consistently. It captures the entire voice chat audio stream and runs continuous transcription, with optional translation into the language you actually read fluently:

  • Group voice chats with multiple simultaneous speakers.
  • Channel voice chats hosted by admins.
  • One-on-one voice and video calls inside Telegram.
  • Recorded voice chats replayed from the channel post.

Audio messages in channels you follow

Many Telegram channels post primarily in voice — news commentators, language teachers, religious figures, sports analysts, and political voices who prefer recording over typing. If you follow channels in a language you only partly understand, those voice posts go from valuable to inaccessible. Live Subtitles fixes that with no preparation: when you tap the play button on a voice message, the audio comes through your system output, Live Subtitles transcribes it, and a translation appears alongside. Long voice messages from channels you follow can also be paused and re-read in the caption history.

Multilingual supergroups and language exchanges

Telegram is famous for huge multilingual supergroups — language-learning communities, expat groups, hobby circles where members come from a dozen countries. When voice chats start, the language mix can be unpredictable. Live Subtitles' dual subtitle mode automatically detects the source language and shows it on the top line plus your translation language on the bottom line, so you can drop into any voice chat in the supergroup and follow along regardless of which language is being spoken at the moment.

No Telegram bot, no channel admin needed

Telegram has a deep bot ecosystem, but bot-based translation has limits: privacy concerns, channel admin permission requirements, and bots that often only see one side of a voice chat. Live Subtitles avoids all of that by being a standalone Windows app reading audio output. It does not appear in the Telegram client, does not interact with the Telegram API, and is invisible to other voice chat participants. Supported recognition and translation languages include English, Russian, Ukrainian, Belarusian, Polish, Bulgarian, Romanian, Greek, Turkish, Persian, Arabic, Hebrew, Hindi, Mandarin Chinese, Japanese, Korean, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, and Indonesian — among 50+ total.
