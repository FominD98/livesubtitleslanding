3. Description of the Service

Live Captions shows speech as text in real time, in the language being spoken: the people talking near you (microphone) or the sound your phone plays (videos, podcasts and other media), optionally in a small window on top of other apps. It labels different speakers in Premium mode and keeps your recent conversations on your phone. The Software does not translate speech.

Premium mode relies on a third-party cloud speech recognition service (Soniox) and requires an internet connection. Offline mode recognizes speech on your phone, with lower accuracy, using open speech models that the Software downloads, or the speech recognition service of your phone for languages without such a model.

Captions are generated automatically and may contain errors, omissions or delays. Do not rely on them as your only source of information in medical, legal, emergency or other situations where a mistake could cause harm.
