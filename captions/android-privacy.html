Privacy Policy

Live Captions for Android · Effective Date: November 25, 2026

Overview

Live Captions ("the App", "we") turns speech into real-time captions in the language being spoken. On Android it can caption the people talking near you (microphone) or the sound your phone itself plays — videos, podcasts and other media (phone audio). This policy covers the Live Captions app for Android (com.livecaptions.android). The App does not require an account. The Windows app has its own Privacy Policy.

The App does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, precise location or photos, shows no ads, does not use advertising identifiers and does not sell your data. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play.

Installation Identifier

To sign requests to our backend, the App derives an identifier for your installation (a one-way hash of the Android ID that the system gives to this app, or a random value) and sends it to our backend. It is an identifier only — it carries no name, email or contact information.

Microphone

Android asks for the microphone permission. The App listens only while captions are running, and only to produce captions. While it listens, Android shows the microphone indicator and the App shows a notification with a Stop button. Android requires the same permission for captioning phone audio.

Phone Audio (Videos and Podcasts)

When you choose "Phone audio", the App captions the sound your phone plays. For this it uses Android's playback capture (MediaProjection), which starts only after you confirm the system dialog — Android shows it every time. The system dialog speaks about recording or casting the screen because that is how Android names this feature; the App takes only the sound. It does not record, view or transmit what is on your screen.

  • The sound is used only to produce captions: in Premium mode it is streamed to our speech recognition provider, in Offline mode it never leaves the phone. We do not record it.
  • Android does not let apps capture phone calls or the sound of most calling apps, and any app can forbid capturing its sound (protected video, for example); the App then gets silence. To caption a call, turn on the speaker and use the microphone.
  • Capture stops when you pause captions, tap the cross on the captions window, tap Stop in the notification or close the App.

Captions are shown only on your screen. Other people in a call do not see them and are not notified. You are responsible for making sure that captioning a conversation is lawful where you are (see our Terms of Service).

Captions Window Over Other Apps

If you allow "Display over other apps", the App shows a small window with captions on top of the video or another app. This permission is used only to draw that window. The App does not read the content of other apps and does not see what you type or tap in them. You can turn the permission off in Android settings at any time; captions are then shown only inside the App.

Offline Mode

In Offline mode speech is recognized on your phone. For English, German, French, Spanish, Russian, Chinese and Korean the App downloads an open speech model once (from GitHub or Hugging Face, which see your IP address as with any download) and then recognizes speech without sending audio anywhere.

For other languages Offline mode uses the speech recognition service installed on your phone (on most phones, Google's). That service is not ours: it handles the audio under its provider's privacy policy and, depending on the phone and the language packs installed, may use the network.

Conversations on Your Phone

The App keeps your recent conversations (text, times and speaker names) on your phone so that you can scroll back, share or delete them. They stay on your phone: we do not upload them and cannot see them. Deleting a conversation, clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App removes them.

Third-Party Services

The following services handle data for us or for you, each under its own privacy policy:

  • Soniox — cloud speech recognition in Premium mode (audio is streamed to it while captions are running)
  • Google Play — purchases, subscriptions and refunds
  • Grafana Loki — diagnostic logs, crash reports and usage events
  • GitHub and Hugging Face — hosting of the speech model files downloaded for Offline mode
  • The speech recognition service of your phone — Offline mode for languages without a downloadable model

Premium mode needs an internet connection. Offline mode works without one after the model has been downloaded.

User Consent

By using the App, you consent to the processing described in this policy, including the transmission of audio to our speech recognition provider while captions are running in Premium mode.

Data Storage and Retention

We do not record or store your audio, and we do not store your captions on our servers. What we retain is limited to: your installation identifier, purchase records (as required for accounting, tax and refunds), diagnostic logs and usage events (for a limited period). App settings, the minute balance and your conversations are stored on your phone.

All data is encrypted in transit.

Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area or the UK, you have the right to request access to your data, request its deletion, and withdraw consent at any time.

Your audio is not stored, and your conversations are only on your phone — you can delete them yourself in the App. For the data we do retain — your installation identifier, logs and usage events — contact us at the address below and we will delete the records associated with your installation, except purchase records we are required to keep by law. For payment data held by Google, use your Google account. You can stop all further processing at any time by uninstalling the App.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

We may update this policy; the Effective Date above will change accordingly.
