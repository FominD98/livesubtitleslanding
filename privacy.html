Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 2, 2026

Overview

Live Captions & Translator respects your privacy. This app is designed to process speech in real time while minimizing data collection.

Information We Process

To provide its functionality, the app may process the following data:

  • Audio input (voice) – captured from your device microphone
  • Transcribed text – generated from your speech
  • Translated text – if translation is enabled
  • Technical data – such as error logs for debugging and performance improvements

We do not store personal data on our servers.

How We Use Information

The processed data is used solely to:

  • Provide real-time captions and translations
  • Improve app performance and stability

Third-Party Services

To provide speech recognition and translation, the app uses third-party services:

  • Google (speech recognition)
  • Yandex (speech recognition)
  • OpenAI (translation)

Audio and text data may be transmitted to these services for processing. These services handle data according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Yandex Privacy Policy
  • OpenAI Privacy Policy

User Consent

By using the app, you consent to the processing of audio and text data as described in this policy, including transmission to third-party services.

Data Storage

We do not store your audio or text data on our servers. Processing is performed in real time.

Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the right to:

  • Request access to your data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time

Since we do not store personal data, we may not be able to provide or delete stored data.

Local Storage

App preferences and settings are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to our servers.
