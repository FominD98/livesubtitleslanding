Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 11, 2026

Overview

Live Subtitles ("the App", "we") turns audio — from your microphone or from audio playing on your device — into real-time captions and translation. This policy covers the Live Subtitles apps for Windows, Android and iOS, as well as this website. The App is designed to process speech in real time while minimizing data collection, and it does not require an account.

Device and Other Identifiers

The App uses identifiers that relate to your device or app installation. In the Google Play Data safety form these are declared under the Device or other IDs data type.

Device fingerprint

To stop the same device from claiming free trial minutes or the rating bonus repeatedly, and to sign requests to our backend, the App derives a stable identifier for your installation and sends it to our backend. On Android it is a SHA-256 hash derived from the Android device identifier (Settings.Secure.ANDROID_ID) combined with the app package name; where that identifier is unavailable, a random per-installation id is generated and used instead. On other platforms it is a stable per-installation identifier stored locally.

This value is an identifier only — it carries no name, email or contact information, and we cannot use it to identify you personally. It is stored on your device and on our backend.

Analytics identifier

Our Android and iOS apps use Google Analytics for Firebase, which assigns an app instance ID (a Firebase installation ID) to your app installation and sends it with analytics events.

No advertising identifiers

We show no ads and do not use advertising identifiers. The Android app explicitly removes the AD_ID permission from its manifest and never reads the Android advertising ID. We do not use your data for advertising, marketing, or personalization, and we do not sell it.

Microphone and System Audio Capture

You choose the audio source for each session. Audio is captured only while a session is active, and only to produce subtitles.

  • Microphone — the App requests microphone permission and captures audio only during a session.
  • System audio — the App captures audio played by your device. On Android this uses MediaProjection's AudioPlaybackCapture and begins only after you grant the system screen-capture consent dialog; DRM-protected audio is muted by the system and cannot be captured. On iOS this uses a Broadcast Upload Extension that you start explicitly. On Windows it uses system loopback capture. Capture stops when you end the session or revoke the permission.

On Android, the App can draw a floating subtitle overlay over other apps. That permission is used only to draw the subtitle window; it does not read other apps' content.

Third-Party Services

Depending on your platform and the recognition or translation engine you select, audio and text may be transmitted to the following processors. Each handles data under its own privacy policy:

  • Deepgram — cloud speech recognition
  • Google — cloud speech recognition and translation (Google Cloud Speech-to-Text and Cloud Translation)
  • Yandex — speech recognition and translation (desktop engine options)
  • OpenAI — translation (desktop engine option)
  • Fish Audio — voice synthesis for the desktop live-voice feature
  • Google Analytics for Firebase — product analytics (mobile apps)
  • Grafana Loki — diagnostic logs and crash reports
  • Google Play Billing and Apple App Store — purchases and subscriptions

On iOS, Apple's on-device speech recognition can be used, and on Windows an offline Whisper model can be downloaded and run locally. In those modes your audio is processed on your device and is not sent to a speech recognition provider.

User Consent

By using the App, you consent to the processing described in this policy, including transmission of audio and text to the third-party services listed above while a session is active.

Data Storage and Retention

We do not record or store your audio, transcripts, or translations on our servers — they are processed in real time and discarded. What we do retain is limited to: your device identifier (for as long as anti-abuse protection requires), diagnostic logs and crash reports (for a limited diagnostic period), analytics events including purchase events (per the retention period configured for our Firebase project), and — on Windows — your minute balance, held against your device identifier. App preferences are stored locally on your device, as is the minute balance on Android and iOS.

All data is encrypted in transit.

Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area or the UK, you have the right to request access to your data, request its deletion, and withdraw consent at any time.

Because we do not store your audio or transcripts, there is nothing of that kind to retrieve or delete. For the data we do retain — your device identifier, diagnostic logs, and analytics events — contact us at the address below and we will delete the records associated with your installation. You can stop all further processing at any time by uninstalling the App.

This Website

live-subtitles.com uses Google Analytics / Google Ads tags, Yandex Metrica and the Meta pixel to measure traffic and campaign performance. These set cookies and collect standard web analytics data, including your IP address and browser information, under their own privacy policies (Google, Yandex, Meta). When you buy minutes on this website, the identifiers the Meta pixel stores in those cookies are sent with the purchase event so we can tell which campaign the purchase came from. This is separate from the data the App processes.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
