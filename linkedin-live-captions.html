LinkedIn Live Captions & AI Translation for Professionals

LinkedIn Live has become the go-to platform for industry keynotes, product launches, panel discussions, and company all-hands — all broadcast to a global professional audience. But LinkedIn provides no built-in live captions for viewers. LinkedIn live captions via Live Subtitles solve this: a floating AI caption overlay appears over your browser as soon as the event starts, with no LinkedIn plugin or browser extension required.

How to add live captions to LinkedIn in 3 steps

  1. Download Live Subtitles from the Microsoft Store and launch the app.
  2. Select "System Audio" as the audio source in Live Subtitles.
  3. Open any LinkedIn Live event or video message in your browser — captions appear automatically in a floating overlay.

HR and people teams running international LinkedIn Live events
