How minutes work

One counter, one rate. Nothing to calculate before you buy.

1 minute = 1 minute

One minute of processed audio costs one minute of balance — the same rate for every recognition and translation engine we support.

Only while it listens

Time is counted only while audio is actually being processed. Having the app open costs nothing.

Plan minutes go first

If you have both, your monthly allowance is spent before any pack minutes, so packs act as a reserve.

No seats to count

Nothing is priced per user or per language pair. Any of the 50+ languages costs the same minute.

Pricing FAQ

Is there a free version?
Yes. You get 20 free minutes when you install the app, plus 10 free Live Voice minutes, with no card required. While you are not on a subscription, your free balance is topped back up to 20 minutes every 30 days.
How is a minute counted?
One minute of processed audio costs one minute of balance, at the same rate for every recognition and translation engine. Time is counted only while audio is actually being processed, and it is rounded up to whole minutes.
Do minute packs expire?
No. Minutes from one-time packs stay on your balance until you use them.
Do subscription minutes roll over?
No. Your monthly allowance is refreshed at the start of each billing period. If you run out early you can add a one-time pack — pack minutes are used only after the subscription allowance is spent.
Is there a free trial of Pro?
Yes. The Pro annual plan starts with 7 days free. Cancel during the trial and you are not charged.
Can I cancel a subscription?
Yes, at any time. You keep your minutes and access until the end of the period you have already paid for. Subscriptions bought in the Microsoft Store, App Store or Google Play are cancelled in that store's account settings.
Why is the price different in my country?
Prices are set per country, and each store applies its own local pricing and taxes. The prices on this page are the lowest US dollar list prices; the exact amount is always shown before you confirm the purchase.
Can I get a refund?
We offer a 14-day refund window for unused or substantially unused purchases — see the Refund Policy. Purchases made in the Microsoft Store, App Store or Google Play are refunded under that store's own policy.
Do you have a plan for teams?
Minutes are not sold per seat, so a large pack or a Max plan usually covers a team. For bulk licences or invoicing, email help@live-subtitles.com.
