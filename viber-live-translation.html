Viber Live Translation & AI Call Captions

Viber is the messaging platform of choice across Eastern Europe, the Balkans, the Philippines, and parts of Southeast Asia — connecting hundreds of millions of users with cross-border family ties. The pattern is consistent: a parent in Bulgaria calling a child working in Germany, a Filipino worker in Saudi Arabia talking to family in Manila, a Ukrainian grandparent reaching grandchildren in Canada. The conversations cross language boundaries every day — and Viber has no built-in real-time translation for voice or video calls. Viber live translation via Live Subtitles fills that gap. Run it alongside Viber on your Windows PC and you get real-time captions plus translation in 50+ languages, with no Viber plugin and no account changes.

How to add live translation to Viber in 3 steps

  1. Download Live Subtitles from the Microsoft Store and launch it alongside Viber for Windows.
  2. Select "System Audio" — captures Viber call audio automatically, no microphone setup.
  3. Make or receive a Viber call — captions and translations appear in a floating overlay on top of Viber.

Cross-border family calls in Eastern Europe

Viber's strongest user base is in Eastern Europe — Russia, Ukraine, Belarus, Bulgaria, Romania, Moldova, Greece, the Czech Republic, Hungary, and across the Balkans. Many of these conversations now happen across languages because of work migration: a parent who only speaks Russian or Ukrainian calling a child who has built a working life in German, English, French, or Polish. Live Subtitles' dual subtitle mode handles this directly. The top line shows the original speech in Russian, Ukrainian, Bulgarian, or Romanian; the bottom line shows a translation into German, English, or whatever the family member uses daily. The grandparent speaks naturally; the working-age relative reads along while still hearing the original voice.

Filipino and Southeast Asian diaspora calls

Viber is also dominant in the Philippines and widely used across Southeast Asia. Filipino workers abroad in the Gulf, Hong Kong, Singapore, the US, and Europe call home daily. When second-generation children grow up speaking the local language more fluently than Tagalog or Cebuano, family calls become bilingual conversations. Live Subtitles supports Filipino/Tagalog, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Thai, Mandarin Chinese, Cantonese, and Korean among its 50+ languages — covering the language pairs Asian diaspora families actually need:

  • Tagalog/Filipino paired with English, Arabic, or Spanish.
  • Vietnamese paired with English, French, German, or Korean.
  • Indonesian or Malay paired with English or Mandarin.
  • Thai paired with English or Mandarin.

Group calls and community channels

Viber communities are gathering places for diaspora groups, hometown networks, religious congregations, and extended family circles. Voice messages and audio shared in communities are often in the home country's language. Live Subtitles captions Viber group calls, Viber Out calls to landline numbers, and any audio message you play back from a community thread — turning a community where you partly understand the audio into one you can fully follow.

No Viber integration, no account permissions

Live Subtitles is a standalone Windows app. It does not log in to your Viber account, does not modify Viber's app or settings, and the other party on the call has no way to know you are using captioning software. This means you can use Live Subtitles for sensitive family conversations, business calls, or community discussions without any disclosure — it is functionally identical to wearing reading glasses while listening. Supported recognition and translation languages include English, Russian, Ukrainian, Belarusian, Polish, Bulgarian, Romanian, Greek, Turkish, Filipino, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Thai, Mandarin Chinese, Cantonese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, Hindi, Portuguese, German, French, Spanish, and Italian — among 50+ total.
