Ad spend per day

Campaigns

matched by campaign name across Google Ads / Meta, GA4 and Paddle

Spend: Google Ads and Meta APIs. New users and buyers: GA4, by the user's first campaign (first touch). GA4 revenue is an estimate. Paddle revenue is exact: utm_campaign passed by the site or the app, or the click id (gclid = Google Ads, fbc = Meta). To get campaigns here: keep Google Ads auto-tagging on, add utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign={{campaign.name}} to Meta ads, and use ?cid=<campaign> on Microsoft Store links (the Windows app carries it into Paddle checkout).
