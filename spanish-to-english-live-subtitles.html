Spanish to English Live Translation — Real-Time AI Subtitles

With over 500 million native speakers, Spanish is the second most spoken language in the world. Whether you're watching Spanish YouTube videos, joining a Zoom call with Latin American colleagues, or learning Spanish as a second language, Spanish to English live subtitles let you follow every word in real time — without pausing, rewinding, or switching apps.

Live Subtitles is a Windows app that uses AI to transcribe Spanish speech and translate it to English instantly. Both the original Spanish text and the English translation appear on screen simultaneously, so you never lose context or miss a word.

How to set up Spanish to English live translation in 3 steps

  1. Download Live Subtitles from the Microsoft Store and launch the app.
  2. Set recognition language to "Spanish" and translation target to "English" in the app settings.
  3. Play any Spanish audio — original Spanish text and its English translation appear on screen simultaneously in real time.

Watch Spanish content with English subtitles — on any platform

Live Subtitles captures your Windows system audio, which means it works with every app on your PC without plugins, browser extensions, or integrations. You get live Spanish to English subtitles on:

  • YouTube — Spanish vlogs, news channels, tutorials, and live streams
  • Netflix — Spanish-language films, series, and documentaries
  • TikTok, Instagram Reels, and other short-form video platforms
  • Spanish podcasts and audio content in any media player
  • Spanish radio or broadcast news streamed in a browser

The floating subtitle overlay stays on top of any window, so you can watch full-screen video and still read every caption.

All Spanish regional accents supported

Spanish is not one accent — it is dozens. The AI in Live Subtitles is trained on a broad range of Spanish speech patterns and recognizes them all accurately:

  • Mexico and Central America — the most widely spoken Spanish variety globally
  • Castilian Spanish (Spain) — including the distinctive "th" pronunciation of certain consonants
  • River Plate Spanish — Argentine and Uruguayan speech with Italian-influenced intonation
  • Caribbean Spanish — Cuban, Puerto Rican, Dominican, and Venezuelan accents
  • Andean Spanish — Colombian, Peruvian, Bolivian, and Ecuadorian regional varieties

You can also select a specific regional Spanish variant in the recognition language settings for even higher accuracy with a particular accent.

Learn Spanish faster with dual subtitles

Intermediate Spanish learners face a classic problem: they know enough Spanish to follow a conversation but still miss words under pressure. The dual subtitle mode in Live Subtitles turns any Spanish content into a language lesson:

  • The top line shows the original Spanish text — exactly what was said
  • The bottom line shows the English translation — so you always understand the meaning
  • Both lines update in real time as the speaker talks

This approach, often called dual-language immersion, is one of the most effective ways to build vocabulary and listening comprehension. Instead of watching Spanish shows with English-only subtitles (which pulls your eyes away from the Spanish), you see both languages simultaneously — your brain makes the connection naturally.
