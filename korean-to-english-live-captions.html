Korean to English live captions solve this gap: Live Subtitles runs on Windows and translates any Korean audio to English in real time, displaying Korean and English side by side on your screen as you watch or listen.

How to set up Korean to English live captions in 3 steps

  1. Download Live Subtitles from the Microsoft Store and launch the app.
