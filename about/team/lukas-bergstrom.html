Lukas Bergström

Real-time Pipelines Engineer, Live Subtitles — based in Stockholm

Lukas owns the meeting-platform integrations: Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, and Webex. His job is to make captions and translations show up where global teams already work, without breaking compliance, recording, or call-quality budgets that enterprise IT cares about.

Background

Lukas studied computer science at KTH Royal Institute of Technology and spent seven years at a Stockholm-based unicorn building WebRTC infrastructure for video calls used in 90+ countries. He moved into captioning when his then-employer's accessibility programme made him responsible for shipping live captions across six languages under a 700-millisecond latency budget. He has been refining that budget ever since.

What Lukas writes about

  • Practical Zoom captioning rollouts for teams that already have a meeting culture they don't want to disrupt.
  • Microsoft Teams compliance, retention, and manager-side workflows.
  • Multilingual Google Meet sessions where half the room speaks one language and the other half doesn't.
  • Honest comparisons of Zoom, Teams, and Google Meet translated captions — including the parts the marketing pages skip.

Editorial standards

Lukas tests every rollout playbook on live meetings before publishing, with at least one trilingual call in the loop. If a recommended setting fails compliance in regulated industries, he flags it. If a competing platform is better for a specific use case, he names it.
