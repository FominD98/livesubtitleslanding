Hiroshi Tanaka

Gaming Overlay Engineer, Live Subtitles — based in Tokyo

Hiroshi is responsible for the parts of Live Subtitles that have to coexist with games: render path, focus stealing, hotkey conflicts, anti-cheat compatibility, and the difference between an overlay that survives a ranked match and one that loses the round for you.

Background

Hiroshi has a degree in information engineering from Keio University and spent six years at a Tokyo-based gaming studio working on rendering and HUD code for two shipped titles. He moved into overlay engineering after building an internal accessibility tool for a competitive title's deaf community in Japan. He plays ranked weekly across two genres, which is how Game Mode keeps acquiring features that real squads actually use.

What Hiroshi writes about

  • Competitive-game subtitle setup that doesn't break under exclusive fullscreen or anti-cheat constraints.
  • Discord voice-chat subtitle workflows for international squads.
  • Per-genre overlay presets (FPS, MOBA, BR, MMO) and the cognitive geometry behind them.
  • Twitch streamer-side workflows for real-time community captions.

Editorial standards

Every gaming guide on this site has been executed against at least two live games in 2026. If a setup behaves differently on Windows 10 vs Windows 11, or on NVIDIA vs AMD GPUs, Hiroshi notes the difference rather than picking a single configuration and ignoring the rest.
